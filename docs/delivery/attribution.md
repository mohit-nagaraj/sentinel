# Attribution and licenses

Sentinel is original assignment code. Its production dependency inventory and
expiring exceptions are validated by `pnpm security:licenses`; see
`security/policy.json` and `docs/security/security-policy.md`.

The evaluation target is the public
[Hi.Events repository](https://github.com/HiEventsDev/Hi.Events) and real
[pull request #1338](https://github.com/HiEventsDev/Hi.Events/pull/1338).
Hi.Events describes its source as AGPL-3.0 with additional attribution terms in
its [project licence](https://github.com/HiEventsDev/Hi.Events/blob/develop/LICENCE).
Sentinel does not redistribute Hi.Events source, binaries, screenshots, or
private data. Committed fixtures contain normalized facts, public paths, commit
identities, and short human-authored descriptions derived from the public PR.

The design and runbook cite current official documentation from Supabase,
Neo4j, Microsoft Azure, GitHub, Render, LangChain, OpenAI, OWASP, and the
respective package/tool projects. Those links are references, not copied
documentation.

No generated image, stock image, private artifact, or third-party logo is
included in the final delivery package. The fallback screenshots show only the
local Sentinel fixture UI.
